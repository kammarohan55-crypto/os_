Why Static Sandboxes Are Insufficient

Traditional sandboxes use fixed policies that cannot adapt to evolving threats. They focus on isolation rather than intelligent protection.

Key Limitations:
  • ❌ Binary decisions without confidence estimation
  • ❌ No learning from analyst feedback
  • ❌ Equal overhead for benign and malicious programs
  • ❌ Detection without explanation
  • ❌ Slow response to threats (detection ≠ containment)
